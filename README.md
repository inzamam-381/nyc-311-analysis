## Key findings

This analysis covers 343,591 NYC 311 requests created from January 1 through February 1, 2026.

- Heat and hot water was the most reported problem, with 77,992 requests (22.7% of the dataset).
- Among the 10 most reported problems, Unsanitary Condition had the longest median time to recorded closure at 495.97 hours (about 20.7 days). Residential Noise had the shortest at 1.14 hours.
- Heat and hot water requests had a median closure time of 50.94 hours. The calculation used 75,556 requests and excluded 2,436 with no closing date.

Closure times measure the interval between request creation and recorded closure. Across the top 10 problem types, 3,146 requests had no closing date and 5 had unusable timestamps. These records were excluded from the timing calculations.
